Who We Are

Bringing stability and simcha to families across Eretz Yisroel.

Simchas Chaim — Joy of Life — was built to prevent families falling apart. Whether it is the impossible weight of Yom Tov expenses, or the silent collapse of a household when illness strikes, we respond quickly, practically, and with complete respect for the family's dignity.

Every allocation is overseen by an independent board of Talmidei Chachamim and respected Baalei Batim, ensuring help is distributed in accordance with the highest standards of Tzedakah.

Two Programs

How We Help

Yom Tov Assistance

Bringing Yom Tov joy to families experiencing financial hardship

Pesach and Sukkos expenses place a heavy demand on a family's budget and becomes almost impossible for those dealing with financial hardship. Simchas Chaim helps 4,000 families across Israel celebrate both respectfully and joyously through grocery support, holiday essentials, and financial aid. These reach each family prior to Yom Tov to ensure that no table remains empty, and no child feels left behind.

Illness Support

Keeping families together through the challenges of serious illness

When a family member falls seriously ill, the entire household is affected. Household chores go undone, children go hungry, and income dwindles just as expenses rise. Simchas Chaim steps in with professional cleaning assistance to restore a sense of normalcy, and grocery cards that allow families to put food on the table with dignity. With these essential needs taken care of, parents can focus on healing, instead of struggling to keep their home from falling apart.
